World Sleep Day falls on March 13th and takes place during Sleep Awareness Week, which runs from March 8 to 14. Together, they create a full week where the world is invited to pause and think about something we do every single night, but often overlook in our daily routines. Anyone who’s spent a stretch of nights staring at the ceiling, or woken up feeling like they never slept at all, knows that feeling well.
