What is Shift Work Sleep Disorder? Shift work disorder (SWSD) is a circadian rhythm sleep disorder that happens when your work schedule forces you to be awake during hours when your body expects to sleep. Around 20% of the full-time workforce in the United States works some form of nontraditional shift, and studies show that 10% to 40% of these workers develop shift work sleep disorder.
