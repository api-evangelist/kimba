Few things are more jarring than waking up from a vivid, frightening dream. Most adults don’t expect to deal with nightmares, so when they do happen, the experience can feel confusing and quite scary. This article breaks down what nightmares are in adults, how common they are, why they happen, and when you should seek professional help.
