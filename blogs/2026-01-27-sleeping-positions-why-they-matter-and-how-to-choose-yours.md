We spend roughly a third of our lives asleep, which means your sleeping position is something your body holds for thousands of hours over a lifetime. How you sleep affects how your spine is loaded, how your joints take pressure, how freely you breathe, and how easily your nervous system stays calm instead of pulling you into light, broken sleep. This guide breaks down why sleeping position matters, how each position affects the body, and how to adjust your setup so your sleep works with you instead of against you.
