How the Olfactory Pathway Influences Sleep To understand how scent can impact the way you sleep, it helps to look at how the brain processes it in the first place. Unlike vision or hearing, which first route through a sensory relay station called the thalamus, smell follows a more direct path. The thalamus filters and organizes most sensory input before it reaches higher brain regions.
