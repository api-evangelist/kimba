Sleep and body weight are more closely linked than most people realize, connected through a cascade of hormonal and neurological changes that operate on multiple simultaneous pathways. In plain words, how you sleep has a direct effect on how your body manages weight. This article breaks down exactly what happens to your body and hormones when you don't get enough sleep, and why those consequences so reliably lead to weight gain.
