Scent has become one of the most talked-about tools in sleep science. But "using scent to sleep better" can mean two very different things depending on the technology behind it. A sleep diffuser and an adaptive scent sleep system might both fill your bedroom with a calming aroma, but how (and when) they do it changes everything about how well they actually work.
