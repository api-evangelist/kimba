Most people associate dreams with REM sleep, but this stage of sleep is also where much of the brain’s emotional and cognitive recovery takes place. When REM sleep is consistently short or fragmented, mood swings, irritability, emotional sensitivity, brain fog, or a sense of mental exhaustion that does not improve with rest are common.
