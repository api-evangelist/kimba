If you’ve ever ramped up your workouts for a race or pushed your training to the max, you might have noticed something odd: instead of sleeping like a baby, you spend the night wide awake. This is because heavy training loads can take a toll on your sleep, which can be frustrating and counterproductive, since quality sleep is when all that hard work truly pays off in muscle repair and recovery. When you don’t get enough solid sleep, growth hormone drops and cortisol rises, meaning you heal slower and won’t reap the full benefits of your training efforts.
