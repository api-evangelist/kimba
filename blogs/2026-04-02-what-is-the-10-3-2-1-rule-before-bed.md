Who knew a handful of numbers could be the thing standing between you and a genuinely good night's sleep? It sounds like a fitness formula or some kind of obscure productivity hack, but the 10:3:2:1 rule is actually one of the better sleep tools out there. The idea behind it is that it takes everything we know about winding down properly and wraps it into a single rule you can actually remember.
