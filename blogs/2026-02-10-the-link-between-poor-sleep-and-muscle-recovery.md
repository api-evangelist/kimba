During intense exercise, muscles experience microscopic tears and stress. It’s during rest – especially deep sleep – that the body repairs these tissues, making them stronger and more resilient. Conversely, poor sleep can short-circuit this recovery process, leaving you sore, weak, or even at higher risk of injury despite all your hard work.
