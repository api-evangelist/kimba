With summers getting hotter and weather patterns becoming increasingly unpredictable, getting a good night's rest during the warmer months feels harder with every passing year. According to sleep researchers, even a modest rise in core body temperature can significantly disrupt sleep architecture, reducing the amount of deep sleep you get. Because of this, you might notice that you toss, turn, and wake up feeling more exhausted than when you went to bed.
