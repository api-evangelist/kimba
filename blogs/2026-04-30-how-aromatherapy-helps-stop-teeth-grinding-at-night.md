What Is Night-Time Teeth Grinding or Sleep Bruxism? Sleep bruxism is the involuntary grinding or clenching of teeth during sleep. It happens when the jaw muscles, mainly the masseter on the side of the jaw and the temporalis at the temple, contract rhythmically and repeatedly through the night.
