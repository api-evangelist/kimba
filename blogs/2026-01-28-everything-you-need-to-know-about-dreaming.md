Dreams are one of the most familiar yet least understood parts of sleep. Almost everyone dreams, yet many people wake up unsure of what happened, when it happened, or why it felt so vivid one night and absent the next. To understand dreaming, we need to look at how the brain behaves during sleep, and what role dreams may play in emotional and cognitive processing.
