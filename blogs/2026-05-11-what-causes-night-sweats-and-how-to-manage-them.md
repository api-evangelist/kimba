If you've ever woken up drenched in sweat in the middle of the night, you're not alone. Night sweats are fairly common, and while they’re often associated with hormonal changes later in life, they can happen at any age and affect all genders. Most causes aren't serious, but identifying what's behind them is the first step to getting a better night's sleep.
