Menopause and perimenopause can make a good night's sleep feel nearly impossible to achieve. This happens because estrogen naturally falls during menopause, destabilizing the body's internal thermostat and sleep-wake cycle, which results in a pattern millions of women experience firsthand: waking up drenched in sweat, staring at the ceiling at 3 a.m. and being unable to fall back to sleep.
