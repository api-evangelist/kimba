For many people, waking up in the middle of the night seems to happen around the same time, often close to 3 AM. You might fall asleep easily at bedtime, only to find yourself wide awake hours later, staring at the ceiling and wondering what went wrong. This kind of early-morning awakening is common, and it doesn’t always mean something is seriously wrong with your sleep.
