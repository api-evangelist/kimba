Sleep paralysis is one of the most unsettling sleep experiences because it feels like waking up while being unable to move. Clinically, it is understood as a temporary overlap between being awake and being in a dream-related sleep state, where awareness returns before the body has fully resumed normal movement. It affects roughly 20 to 30 percent of people at least once in their lifetime and, although it can feel frightening, it is not physically dangerous in otherwise healthy individuals.
