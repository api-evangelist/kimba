Most people think sleep deprivation looks like falling asleep at your desk or struggling to keep your eyes open on the highway, but the reality is far more subtle, and far more common. Millions of people walk around every day running on too little sleep, genuinely believing they’re fine, because they’ve been tired for so long that tired has become their new normal. Even though your body can adapt and keep you functioning, the warning signs are still there, often hiding in plain sight.
